finx-slide draws and edits PowerPoint decks with BetterOffice (Apache-2.0), used from our fork
github.com/Eliseowzy/betteroffice. Its LICENSE and NOTICE are kept here as they ship. With the
`fonts-latin` pack the engine embeds Latin font faces and a few decoders; their licenses, copied
from the fork's crates/betteroffice-fonts-latin/LICENSES, are in fonts-latin/. CJK faces are
read from the machine at run time and are not distributed.
